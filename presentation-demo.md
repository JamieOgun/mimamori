# MimaMori Demo Narrative

## Slide 1: Opening

MimaMori is a caretaker AI agent that calls an elderly loved one every day, has a natural conversation, and flags possible signs of mental decline to a caregiver or doctor.

## Slide 2: The Problem

This project comes from my own personal experience.

My grandma has started experiencing **boke**, a common Japanese word for age-related mental decline. She forgets things more easily now, and small changes in memory, mood, or daily routine can be hard to notice unless someone talks with her regularly.

The problem is that I care about her deeply, but I am also busy. I cannot reliably call her every day.

## Slide 3: Why This Matters

Mental decline often shows up in small signals:

- Repeating the same story or question
- Confusion about time, dates, or recent events
- Shorter or less coherent responses
- Lower mood, anxiety, or withdrawal
- Changes in appetite, sleep, or daily routine

These signs are easy to miss when family members are busy or live far away.

## Slide 4: The Idea

What if an AI agent could make the daily check-in call for me?

Not to replace family, doctors, or caregivers, but to create a consistent daily touchpoint.

The goal is simple:

- Call my grandma every day
- Have a warm, natural conversation
- Capture the transcript
- Score cognitive and emotional signals
- Flag concerning changes to a caregiver or doctor

## Slide 5: Product Demo

In the dashboard, I can:

- Start a demo call immediately
- Set a recurring daily call time
- Review recent call transcripts
- See cognitive and emotional scores
- Open score breakdowns for more detail
- Identify calls that need follow-up

## Slide 6: How It Works

1. A daily schedule triggers the outbound call.
2. Twilio places the phone call.
3. The AI agent speaks with the recipient in a warm caretaker persona.
4. The conversation is transcribed.
5. The transcript is scored for cognitive and emotional signals.
6. The dashboard shows the call record, transcript, risk level, and score breakdown.

## Slide 7: What The AI Looks For

Cognitive signals:

- Repetition
- Temporal confusion
- Word finding difficulty
- Confabulation or contradictions
- Vocabulary complexity
- Response coherence

Emotional signals:

- Affect
- Anxiety or agitation
- Social withdrawal
- Interest in activities
- Overall mood

## Slide 8: Why This Is Different

Most eldercare tools are passive: alarms, sensors, or dashboards that wait for something obvious to happen.

MimaMori is proactive.

It creates a daily conversation, then turns that conversation into structured insight for the people responsible for care.

## Slide 9: Safety And Positioning

MimaMori is not a diagnosis tool.

It is a monitoring and early-warning assistant. The AI flags patterns that may deserve attention, but doctors and caregivers make the actual care decisions.

The system should only call people who have given consent, and alerts should be reviewed by a human before any medical conclusion is made.

## Slide 10: Vision

The long-term vision is to help families stay close to elderly loved ones, even when daily life gets busy.

MimaMori gives caregivers a consistent signal:

Is grandma doing okay today?

And if something starts to change, someone can notice earlier.

## Demo Closing

I built MimaMori because this is a real problem in my own family.

I wanted something that could help me check in every day, preserve a human-like conversation, and make it easier to involve caregivers or doctors when the signs become concerning.
